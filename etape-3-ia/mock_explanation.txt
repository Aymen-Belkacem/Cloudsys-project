### 2.2 EXCEPTIONAL LOGISTICS COSTS — CONFIRMED SECONDARY CAUSE

#### Maritime freight:

- **Baltic Dry Index (May 2023):** ~1,100-1,200 points (vs. 5-year average: ~800)
- **FRA→EGY freight (wheat):** estimated 45-55 USD/t in May 2023 (vs. 25-30 USD/t in normal period)
- **Freight surcharge:** +20-25 USD/t = **+5-6% of FOB price of 400 USD/t**

#### War risk insurance premiums:

- **Standard rate (2022-2023):** 0.5-1.0% of value (classic insurance)
- **War risk premium Mediterranean/Suez (May 2023):** +1.5-2.5% additional
- **Impact:** +6-10 USD/t on 400 USD/t = **+1.5-2.5%**

#### Rerouting and delays:

- Avoiding Suez/Black Sea → detour via Gibraltar/Cape of Good Hope: **+8-12 days**
- Additional fuel cost + vessel immobilization: +8-12 USD/t = **+2-3%**

**Subtotal logistics costs: +35-45 USD/t = +8.75-11.25% of FOB price**

#### Theoretical CIF cost recap:

```
FOB Price (FRA)              : 400.0 USD/t
+ Maritime freight           : +50.0 USD/t (12.5%)
+ Classic insurance          : +4.0 USD/t (1.0%)
+ War risk premium           : +8.0 USD/t (2.0%)
+ Handling/port fees         : +20.0 USD/t (5.0%)
= Theoretical CIF            : 482.0 USD/t
```

**Theoretical gap: 482 vs 600 observed = 118 USD/t (24.5%) not explained by logistics alone.**

→ **This residual gap reflects Egypt's perceived demand/scarcity premium (supply/demand tension).**
